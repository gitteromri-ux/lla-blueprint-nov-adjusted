Cake 4:5 / 1:1 band-lift method (Gita E-commerce). Source cake_4k_final4.mp4 (2160x3840).
Works ONLY where the background behind the bands is uniform (Cake slide on black). On full-bleed Julie footage (Sugar/Red Wine) restacking bands creates a visible seam; captions there are baked at y~3260-3700, hook y~590-920.
Section times: opening 0-3.95s, body 3.95-(dur-7), end card dur-7..end. Measure bands per clip with bright-row scan.
